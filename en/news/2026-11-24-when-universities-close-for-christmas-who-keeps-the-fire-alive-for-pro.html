Italy: When universities close for Christmas, who keeps the fire alive for prospective students?

24 November 2026 · EuroPath Education · Admissions

From tearful January intake applicants to university offers issued on Christmas Day, Karen Claffey from Uarc explains why festive admissions support isn't about call volumes – it's about being there when it matters most. The post When universities close for Christmas, who keeps the fire alive for prospective students? appeared first on The PIE News .

What happened

Speaking from lived experience covering the Christmas period with Uarc , Karen Claffey, business development manager, spoke to The PIE , saying &#8220;we&#8217;ve answered calls from students in tears, worried about their January start date and convinced they won&#8217;t be able to speak to anyone until the New Year. &#8220;Some are genuinely surprised when someone picks up the phone. We&#8217;ve even been asked whether we&#8217;re AI. There is a sense of relief that we are real people because sometimes that&#8217;s exactly what a worried student needs. &#8220;Our team offer reassurance, help applicants understand their next steps and, crucially, escalate urgent enquiries directly to university colleagues through agreed channels when necessary.

For universities, that continuity of support is particularly valuable for international applicants navigating unfamiliar processes, time zones and deadlines. Often in the student journey one of the most valuable interactions a university can offer over Christmas is simply being there, because there is an expectation that they might not.
