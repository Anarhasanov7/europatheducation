Our mission
What we do
We are based in Italy and work with universities and government offices in person — not remotely.

Mission of Edupath Education

We help international graduates access Italian and French higher education — from document submission to diploma. We specialize in regional scholarships DSU/EDISU, covering tuition, scholarships up to €7,000/year, dormitory, and meals. We support the student throughout the entire study period, not just at admission.

Benefits
What each side gets
Partnership with Edupath benefits everyone — the university, the student, and the partner.
