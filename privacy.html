Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how EuroPath Education ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and application ("the Service"). The Service provides educational consulting for international students seeking to study in Italy and publishes educational content to social media platforms.

1. Information We Collect

Contact information: When you submit a consultation request or contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, country, and any information you provide in your message.

Account information: If you create an account, we store your email address and authentication credentials.

Usage data: We collect anonymous analytics data such as page views, visitor location (country-level), and browser type to improve our Service.

Social media tokens: For our automated content publishing, we store OAuth access tokens for connected social media accounts (TikTok, Threads, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube). These tokens are stored securely and used only to publish educational content.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

• Respond to your consultation requests and inquiries
• Provide educational guidance about studying in Italy
• Publish educational content to our social media accounts
• Improve our website and services
• Send relevant updates about Italian universities and scholarships (with your consent)

3. Social Media Platform Integration

Our Service integrates with TikTok, Threads, Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube via their official APIs. When we publish content to these platforms, the platform operators may process data according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these platforms.

For TikTok specifically, our app uses the Content Posting API to publish educational videos. TikTok access tokens are stored securely and used solely for publishing content to the authorized TikTok account.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure (Supabase, Cloudflare). Access tokens and sensitive credentials are encrypted at rest. We use row-level security policies to ensure that user data is only accessible to authorized users.

5. Data Retention

We retain consultation requests and contact submissions for as long as necessary to provide our services. Social media tokens are retained until the connected account is disconnected or the token expires.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

• Access the personal data we hold about you
• Request correction of inaccurate data
• Request deletion of your personal data
• Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
• Object to direct marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@europatheducation.eu.

7. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for analytics and authentication. We do not sell your data to third parties or use it for targeted advertising.

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

• Supabase (database hosting)
• Cloudflare (web hosting and CDN)
• Google Analytics (anonymous usage statistics)
• Resend (transactional email)
• TikTok, Meta (Facebook/Instagram/Threads), YouTube (social media publishing)

9. Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for individuals aged 16 and older who are interested in studying in Italy. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by posting the updated policy on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@europatheducation.eu.
